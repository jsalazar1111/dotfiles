## 2. Typography

### Font Family: Open Sans (exclusively)

| Element    | Weight/Style          | Size (presentations) | Size (documents)  |
|------------|-----------------------|----------------------|-------------------|
| Titles     | Open Sans Semi Bold   | 36-44pt              | 24-28pt           |
| Subtitles  | Open Sans Light       | 20-24pt              | 16-18pt           |
| Body text  | Open Sans Regular     | 14-16pt              | 10-12pt           |
| Captions   | Open Sans Regular     | 10-12pt              | 8-9pt             |

### Typography Rules
- Open Sans is the ONLY approved font
- Do not use Fjalla One (this font is being phased out entirely)
- Do not use Arial as a primary font. Arial is only acceptable as a system fallback when Open Sans cannot be embedded or loaded
- Do not choose random or decorative fonts under any circumstances
- For web/HTML outputs, load Open Sans from Google Fonts: `https://fonts.googleapis.com/css2?family=Open+Sans:wght@300;400;600&display=swap`
  - Weight 300 = Light (subtitles)
  - Weight 400 = Regular (body)
  - Weight 600 = Semi Bold (titles)

## 3. Logo Usage

### Logo Variants
The Plative logo consists of "The Lantern" icon (a stylized location pin in Crimson) and the "PLATIVE" wordmark in spaced capitals.

Three configurations exist:
- **Horizontal:** Lantern to the left of the wordmark, side by side
- **Vertical:** Lantern above the wordmark, stacked
- **Symbol only:** The Lantern icon alone (minimum size: 10px)

### Background Rules
| Background   | Lantern Color | Wordmark Color | Notes                           |
|-------------|---------------|----------------|---------------------------------|
| White/Light | Crimson       | Black/Navy     | Default, most common usage       |
| Dark/Navy   | Crimson       | White          | For dark slides and backgrounds  |

### What NOT to Do
- Never use an all-white, all-black, or all-crimson version of the logo
- Never alter proportions of any part of the logo
- Never angle the wordmark
- Never add effects (drop shadow, stroke, glow)
- Never add new fonts to the logo
- Never place on busy backgrounds or clashing colors
- Never remove parts of the logo or the lantern icon
- Never change the logo colors or transparency

### Clear Space
Always leave adequate white space around the logo borders. No other elements should encroach on the logo's breathing room.

### Minimum Sizes
- Symbol only: 10px
- Horizontal: 15px height
- Vertical: 21px height

## 4. Layout Patterns

### Two-Panel Layout (Signature Plative Pattern)
This is the most distinctive Plative layout. Used on section intro slides and content-heavy pages:
- **Left panel (~40% width):** Dark navy blue (#262A42) background with a subtle aurora/gradient overlay (warm tones fading from top-right). Title text in white, left-aligned. A short gold accent line may appear under the title.
- **Right panel (~60% width):** White or very light background with content in Navy Blue text.

### Slide Types

**Title/Cover Slides:**
- Full-bleed graphic background (mountains, prairie, forest, city, or gradient)
- White title text, large (40-52pt Open Sans Semi Bold)
- White subtitle text below (Open Sans Light)
- Plative logo centered or bottom-right
- Crimson lantern + white wordmark

**Section Divider Slides:**
- Use the two-panel layout
- Left panel: Navy/gradient with white title text
- Right panel: white, can be blank or contain navigation/TOC
- Or: full-bleed graphic from approved assets

**Content Slides:**
- White background
- Navy Blue (#262A42) title at top
- Body text in Navy or dark gray
- Plative logo bottom-right (Crimson lantern + black/navy wordmark)
- Page number next to logo, separated by a thin vertical line

**Data/Chart Slides:**
- Use gradient backgrounds (Gradient 3, 4, or 5) for chart-heavy slides
- Ensure font is large enough to read against the gradient
- Use Gradient 5 (light) if imagery is complex and dark backgrounds would be distracting

### Footer Pattern (Presentations)
- Bottom-right: Plative logo (horizontal, small)
- Thin vertical line separator
- Page number to the right of the line

## 5. CTA / Button Styling

### On Light/White Backgrounds (approved fills)
- Navy Blue (#262A42) fill + white text
- Teal (#0D9DA4) fill + white text
- Deep Teal (#005957) fill + white text
- Crimson (#E14136) fill + white text
- Gold (#DEAB57) fill + Navy text

### On Dark Backgrounds (approved fills)
- Soft Teal (#9CDAE6) fill + Navy text
- Gold (#DEAB57) fill + Navy text
- Crimson (#E14136) fill + white text
- White fill + Navy text
- Teal (#0D9DA4) fill + white text

### CTA Don'ts
- No Soft Teal buttons on white backgrounds (low contrast)
- No Navy text on Navy backgrounds
- No Crimson text on Crimson backgrounds
- No light text on light backgrounds
- No text-only CTAs without a fill

## 6. Contrast Rules

### Approved Combinations

**Dark backgrounds (Navy, Deep Teal, dark gradients):**
- White text
- Soft Teal text (for secondary info)
- Gold text (for accents)

**Light backgrounds (White, Soft Teal, light gradients):**
- Navy Blue text
- Crimson text (for emphasis only, not body)
- Deep Teal text

### Forbidden Combinations
- Dark text on dark backgrounds
- Light text on light backgrounds
- Crimson body text on white (Crimson is for accent/emphasis only, not paragraphs)
- The old "Corona Red" on any background

## 7. Graphic Assets

Plative has a library of approved background graphics. These are landscape illustrations and gradients used for slide backgrounds. When creating outputs programmatically (where actual image files cannot be used), approximate the feel with:

### Full-Bleed Backgrounds (for title/transition slides)
- **Mountains 1 & 2:** Low-poly mountain landscape, dark navy/purple tones with warm aurora colors at top
- **Prairie:** Open landscape with warm/cool gradient sky
- **Forest:** Dense forest silhouette with atmospheric depth
- **City:** Urban skyline silhouette

### Border Variants (for content slides)
- **Mountain Border, Prairie Border, Forest Border, City Border:** The graphic occupies roughly 30-40% of the slide (typically left or bottom), leaving the rest white for content

### Gradients
- **Gradient 1:** Dark navy bottom-left fading to warm aurora top-right. For end slides, transitions, large callouts.
- **Gradient 2:** Similar warm-to-cool gradient. For callouts and medium copy.
- **Gradient 3:** Blue gradient. For charts and dense content (if font is large enough).
- **Gradient 4:** Simplified blue gradient without aurora. For complex imagery where aurora distracts.
- **Gradient 5:** Light background gradient. For complex imagery where dark backgrounds distract.

### Approximating Backgrounds in Code
When you cannot embed actual image files, create CSS/SVG gradients that echo the Plative palette:
```css
/* Dark aurora-style gradient (approximates Mountains/Gradient 1) */
background: linear-gradient(135deg, #262A42 0%, #425582 40%, #DEAB57 80%, #E14136 100%);

/* Navy to soft navy gradient (approximates Gradient 4) */
background: linear-gradient(180deg, #262A42 0%, #425582 100%);

/* Light teal gradient (approximates Gradient 5) */
background: linear-gradient(180deg, #FFFFFF 0%, #9CDAE6 100%);
```

## 8. Iconography

Plative uses a consistent set of line-style icons in Navy Blue (#262A42) on light backgrounds. The icons are simple, outlined (not filled), and uniform in stroke weight.

When creating outputs that need icons, use a matching icon library (e.g., Lucide, Feather, or similar thin-line icon sets) in Navy Blue or white depending on background.

Categories of approved icon concepts: Registration, Calendar, Event, Email, Documents, Laptop, Settings, Global, Upload, Notification, Certified, Apps, Bolt, Server, Rocket, Framework, Alert, Check, Cloud, Secure, Code, Search, Home, Collaboration, Alignment, Trophy, and more.

## 9. Common Mistakes to Avoid

1. Using Fjalla One font (retired entirely)
2. Using Arial as a primary font (only as a fallback)
3. Using the old "Corona Red" instead of Crimson #E14136
4. Dark text on dark backgrounds (violates contrast rules)
5. Light text on light backgrounds (violates contrast rules)
6. All-white, all-black, or all-crimson logo versions
7. Placing the logo on busy or clashing backgrounds
8. Adding effects to the logo (shadows, strokes, glows)
9. Using random fonts instead of Open Sans
10. Forgetting the logo on deliverables
11. Using Crimson for body text (accent only)
12. Low-contrast CTAs (like Soft Teal button on white)
