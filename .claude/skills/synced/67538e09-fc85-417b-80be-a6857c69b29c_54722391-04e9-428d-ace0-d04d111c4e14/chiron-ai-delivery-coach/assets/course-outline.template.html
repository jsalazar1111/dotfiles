Meet Chiron, your agentic delivery coach

In Greek myth, Chiron was the centaur who trained heroes like Achilles, Jason and Asclepius. He taught practical skills, from medicine and archery to strategy, and the judgment to use them well.

Chiron steps you through setting up your AI tools, working the Plative way with Claude Code, VS Code and GitHub, and building real Salesforce or NetSuite configurations in a practice org.

After that, Chiron stays with you as your personal agentic delivery coach. Bring it your day-to-day delivery questions, and it helps you find the best path forward.

Say it KY-ron, like Byron.

Course Outline

Your guided path from your first Claude Code session to a live agentic build on Salesforce or NetSuite. Go at your own pace: you can stop anytime and pick up where you left off. This page updates as you go.

1

Start

{{TITLE_1}}

To start your learning session, type /chiron-ai-delivery-coach in Claude Code or claude.ai. Use the same command to come back anytime.

You tell Chiron whether you work in Salesforce, NetSuite or both. Then it walks you through opening a terminal, installing Claude Code if needed, and signing in with your Plative account.

3

Get comfortable

{{TITLE_3}}

You open a small practice project in VS Code, look around, and start Claude Code in VS Code's built-in terminal. Chiron shows you every click.

First, your model and effort

  • You use Sonnet at low effort as your everyday setting.
  • You learn which model suits which job: Haiku for quick lookups, Sonnet for everyday building, and Opus for planning, hard analysis and tricky problems.
  • You raise the effort only when the work is hard. The bottom line of your Claude window always shows the model and effort in use.
4

Git and GitHub basics

{{TITLE_4}}

You create a private practice project on GitHub, called a repository or "repo". Every project at Plative is private, so only the people you choose can see it. Then you learn the everyday moves: saving versions of your work, comparing two versions, and trying a change on a copy before you keep it. You also see why GitHub doubles as your backup, and how Git can follow a project's stages, like Build, QA, UAT and Production.

8

Record and share

{{TITLE_8}}

You pick the exercises to show. Chiron suggests a mix, drafts a short summary of each, and coaches you through four parts: set the scene, let Claude build, show it works, and wrap up. Then you rehearse, record, and share it in #ai-native-plative on Slack.

Once it is shared, you can choose to clean up what you built in the sandbox, so it stays tidy for others.

9

Level up

{{TITLE_9}}

  • Read the bottom line of your Claude window. It shows your model and effort, how full your session is, how much of your allowance you have used, and whether your work is saved to GitHub.
  • Give Claude a project guide. Type /init and Claude writes CLAUDE.md, a short guide to your project and its rules that every new session reads first, so you stop repeating yourself.
  • Tidy up before the session gets full. When the part showing how full your session is (like 120K/1.0M) turns orange, ask Claude to write down what you found, what you decided and what comes next. Then type /compact to shrink the session and carry on.
  • Send a helper to explore. For big reading jobs, such as going through all your flows, ask Claude to send a helper. Your main session stays focused.
  • Go back a step. If Claude heads the wrong way, type /rewind (or press Esc twice) and pick an earlier message. You can undo the conversation, the changes to your files, or both. It only undoes changes on your computer, not anything already sent to your sandbox or GitHub.
  • Come back to a session. Closed a window by mistake? Type /resume to pick up an earlier session, or run claude --continue to reopen the last one.
  • Carry on from your phone. Type /rc and scan the code with your phone to follow and steer the same session from the Claude app while your computer keeps working.

10

Coaching and support

{{TITLE_10}}

Once your demo is shared, Chiron becomes your coach for day-to-day delivery work. Bring it a question or a problem from a real project. Chiron tells you what to do next, including the exact words to give your own Claude Code session.

When it helps to see the problem, Chiron offers to look. It looks only after you say yes.

Your browserA tab you already have open.
A screenshotYour screen, after a one-time setting change.
A session reviewA Claude Code session you share by its ID from /status.

Chiron sees only what you already see. It never clicks, saves, or types for you, and never changes your files or your orgs. You stay in charge of your work.
