## Core Differentiators

These are the reasons Salesforce AEs bring Plative into deals. Use these as the backbone of any positioning output.

### 1. AI-Native Outcome-Based Delivery

Most SIs use AI as a marketing claim. Plative builds it into every phase of actual delivery. Before a project starts, we run automated metadata extracts and Hubbl org scans to produce a data-backed picture of the customer's environment. During delivery, we use automated backlog discovery, CLI-based and Claude Code-assisted Salesforce configuration, and automated testing pipelines. This means our teams spend less time on low-value configuration work and more time on architecture, decisions, and outcomes.

The practical result for customers: fewer billable hours, tighter SOWs, and more outcome-based engagements where Plative's success is tied to measurable results, not time spent. For Salesforce AEs, it means the engagements you co-sell with Plative close faster, run cleaner, and expand because the customer saw what they were promised.

Plative holds partnerships with Anthropic, Snowflake, and AWS specifically to bring this capability across the full customer tech stack, not just inside the Salesforce org.

### 2. Summit Status With Senior Delivery

Plative holds Salesforce's highest partner tier. More importantly, we staff senior practitioners on customer engagements, not delivery coordinators managing offshore teams the customer never meets. The people who sell the engagement are connected to the people who deliver it.

### 3. Dual Salesforce and NetSuite Practice

Very few SIs hold Summit-level status on Salesforce and Top-3 status on NetSuite simultaneously. For any customer whose transformation touches CRM and ERP (which is most mid-market and enterprise transformations), Plative eliminates the coordination gap between two separate SIs. This is a direct co-sell advantage for Salesforce AEs whose accounts also have NetSuite conversations in motion.

### 4. FSC Depth That Generalist SIs Cannot Match

Plative has delivered FSC Core implementations for wealth management, private credit, and alternative investment firms. We understand custodian integrations (Schwab, Fidelity, Tamarac, Addepar), household models, complex entity hierarchies, Spring '26 Flexible Hierarchies, ARC (Actionable Relationship Center), Data Processing Engine, Party Relationship Groups, and the full financial services data model. Most SIs sell FSC. Few can staff it at the level these customers require.

### 5. Agentforce Production Experience

Plative has deployed 50+ custom Agentforce agents in production across wealth management, asset management, real estate, nonprofit, and high-tech customers. We have a proprietary accelerator called AgentSave specifically designed to drive adoption and measurable outcomes for Agentforce customers who have licensed but not yet realized value. AgentSave is outcomes-based: 30% of the fee is tied to results. This directly supports Salesforce AE consumption growth.

### 6. PE Ecosystem Access

Plative has active operating partner relationships across major PE firms including Riverside, KKR, PSG, Audax, Summit Partners, Vector Capital, and Gridiron Capital. For Salesforce AEs working PE-backed accounts, Plative is already known and trusted in those sponsor communities. We understand TSA deadlines, value creation plans, carve-out timelines, and what an operating partner needs from a CRM implementation.

### 7. Hubbl Org Diagnostics

Before scoping any existing-org engagement, Plative runs a Hubbl diagnostic that produces a scored org health report. This gives both Plative and the Salesforce AE a credible, data-backed picture of org debt, unused licenses, redundant automations, and quick win opportunities. It accelerates trust with the customer and tightens the SOW so there are no surprises at delivery.

### 8. Global Delivery at Competitive Rates

Plative has delivery capacity in Mumbai and Manila alongside its North American teams in New York and Toronto. Customers get senior NA-based architects and project leadership with a blended delivery rate that mid-market budgets can absorb. This is the answer to the objection that a Summit Partner will be too expensive.

### 9. We Protect the AE's Customer Relationship

Plative treats the Salesforce AE's customer relationship as shared. We communicate proactively, stay on scope, and do not create noise that lands back on the AE's desk. Salesforce AEs who have co-sold with Plative consistently renew and expand those accounts. Clean delivery protects the renewal.

## Common Objections and Responses

### "Why not Accenture or Deloitte?"

The honest answer: those firms win on brand for very large enterprise deals where the customer wants a household name on the engagement letter. For mid-market and growth-stage companies ($50M-$2B revenue), they over-resource, under-deliver, and leave the customer with a team of junior staff after the partners sell the deal. Plative's senior practitioners are on the engagement from kickoff through go-live. The people who sold it are reachable when something goes sideways.

### "We have never heard of Plative."

Plative is a Summit Partner, Salesforce's highest partner tier. We have delivered for Rectangle Health, iCapital, Savant Wealth Management, REBNY, SchoolStatus, NinjaOne, and others. We are intentionally mid-market focused, which means we are not competing for the JPMC deals. We are competing for the accounts in your book that a big SI would staff with their most junior team anyway.

### "Another SI told us this would cost half as much."

If the number is dramatically lower, either the scope is wrong or the delivery model is offshore-heavy with a thin onshore veneer. Plative runs Hubbl diagnostics before scoping. Our numbers reflect what the work actually takes. We have seen too many implementations blow past a low initial number by 40-60% because the scope was optimistic. We would rather set the right expectation upfront.

### "The customer had a bad Salesforce implementation before."

This is actually the best signal for Plative. Hubbl diagnostics surface exactly what went wrong in prior implementations. We know how to scope remediation work with specificity and how to rebuild trust with a skeptical customer. We have done this in private credit, wealth management, and tech.

### "The customer wants to use their current Salesforce licenses more efficiently before adding scope."

That is the AgentSave conversation. Run a Hubbl diagnostic, identify what is unused or broken, and come back with a tight remediation plan. Plative can start there and grow the engagement naturally.
