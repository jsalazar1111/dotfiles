# FSM: Installation and Commission — Discovery Questions

*Source: Discovery Questions Combined (NetSuite implementation discovery bank) — Field Service Management use case*


## Use Case Metrics

- Business Importance
- No. Jobs per week/month
- % of FSM business in terms of qty
- % of FSM business in terms of revenue
- How long do installation projects run for on average?

## Resourcing Metrics

- How many internal field staff work on this use case?
- Are subcontractors also used?
- If yes, will subcontractors be using the Field Service Mobile?
- Are these staff shared across other FSM use cases and if so which ones?
- What different staff roles will be required to use the Field Service mobile?

## Sales Process & Pricing

- Capture End to End Process.  If customers have documented processes include them here e.g
- How are orders received
- How are orders processed
- What is on the order (request examples)
- Are there items on the order that will become assets in the field?
- Are there items on the order that will already exist as assets?
- If so what is the maximum number of assets and average sold on an order?
- Are the assets generally in stock when placed on the sales order or are they often special ordered or manufactured?
- Is there a desire to create the assets automatically at fulfilment from the sales order?
- Are all the assets on a sales order installed at the same location?
- How and when is the sales order invoiced?
- Is there a warranty on any of the assets sold on the sales order?
- Are there any other service agreements associated with the sales order for the assets?
- Are there items on the Sales Order than need to be consumed/drawn down on over the life of the project?

## Assets

- What types of assets are included in this sales process?
- Are many assets installed on a project?
- If Yes, what is the maximum number of assets installed on a project?
- What is the average?
- Are all assets installed at the same time, or are they staggered over the length of the project?
- What information about the assets is required to be captured at point of creation?  (request examples)
- What information about the assets is required to be captured in the field for future use?  (request examples)
- Do the Assets need to be grouped by their location/site?
- Do the Assets have components that need to be tracked?

## Asset Warranty

- When assets are sold do they have any type of warranty?
- If Yes, capture the details of each warranty including duration, coverage and application
- Warranties can be complex so take time to capture the detail.

## FSM Scheduling Process

- At what point in the end to end sales process are field staff required to be scheduled work?
- Who schedules this work?
- How is the determined what work needs to be scheduled?
- How is it decided who will be scheduled to do the work?
- For what periods of time are field staff scheduled into the field for a project?
- Are staff scheduled as part of teams?
- What information do you need to see on the schedule board?
- Are there any notifications required to be sent when scheduling?

## FSM In the Field Process
Additional Roles or permissions may be out of scope

- Role 1 - eg installer
- How long does this role spend working on the project and when?
- Does this role work as a member of a team?
- What Contact information does the person need to see on the mobile?
- Does the role need to create Assets in the field?
- Does the role need to update existing Assets in the field?
- Does the role need to complete any installation/commissioning checklists in the field?
- Does the role need to complete any other checklists in the field e.g safety?
- Does the role need to capture their time in the field?
- Does the role need to capture parts usage from their van in the field?
- Does the role need to capture any expenses in the field?
- Does the role need to capture images/photos in the field?
- Does the role need to capture any other information in the field eg arrival and departure times, issues, etc?
- Are Signatures required to be captured in the field at the completion of the task/job?
- Is there a requirement to view the Service Report from the mobile?
- Is there a requirement to send the Service Report from the mobile?
- Are there specific contacts that the user needs to be able to see on the mobile?

## Job Completion

- Do Installation Projects have phases or milestones?
- When is the Instllation Job completed?
- Who completes the Installation Job and closes it down?
- Is there a review stage for any completed works for the project prior to invoicing that needs to be recorded from the mobile?

## Service Reports

- Are service reports required to be generated for this use case? (request examples)
- If so, what reports and what information is to be presented
- Who do Service Reports need to be delivered to?

## Service History

- What information captured from this use case is required to be visible on the Service History on the mobile in the future?

## Invoicing & Revenue

- When is the project invoiced?
- Is any of the work completed in the field via the mobile required to be invoiced e.g time is invoiced, parts installed, or is it all on the sales order already.
