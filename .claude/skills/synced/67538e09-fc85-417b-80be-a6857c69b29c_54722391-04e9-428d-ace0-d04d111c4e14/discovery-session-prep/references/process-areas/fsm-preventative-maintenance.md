# FSM: Preventative Maintenance — Discovery Questions

*Source: Discovery Questions Combined (NetSuite implementation discovery bank) — Field Service Management use case*


## Use Case Metrics

- Business Importance
- No. Jobs per week/month
- % of FSM business in terms of qty
- % of FSM business in terms of revenue
- How long do Maintenance jobs run for on average?

## Resourcing Metrics

- How many internal field staff work on this use case?
- Are subcontractors also used?
- If yes, will subcontractors be using the Field Service Mobile?
- Are these staff shared across other FSM use cases and if so which ones?
- What different staff roles will be required to use the Field Service mobile? e.g installers, site managers etc.

## Sales Process, Pricing & Billing

- Capture End to End Process.  If customers have documented processes include them here e.g
- Do you sell maintenance contracts?  If so, provide the common examples including coverage, pricing, frequencies, assets covered, invoicing, duration
- What is on the sales order for a Service Contract (request examples)
- How and when are the maintenance contracts invoiced?
- Is anything captured in the field invoiced to the customer, or is everything for the maintenance covered under the contract itself?
- Are there any other terms on the service contracts that impact the pricing of other job types e.g breakfix
- Do you also offer adhoc maintenance that are one offs, but not part of a contract?

## Assets

- What types of assets are covered under Service Contracts?
- Are many assets serviced under a PM maintenance contract?
- If Yes, what is the maximum number of assets serviced on a job?
- What is the average approximately? (request examples)
- Are different assets serviced at different frequencies under the contracts?
- Does more than one person service the assets on a job?
- If more than one, do different assets get serviced by different people, and if so, what are the rules?
- Are there service contracts based on recurrences other than time frequencies (e.g machine hours, km) - NOTE: that Field Service does not have an automated solution for anything by time frequency based maintenance

## Contract Details (continuing from question on sales contracts above)

- Are the frequencies complex e.g minor monthly alternating, or similar
- Are the requirements for the tasks created complex e.g need to set days of week, times of day, etc.
- Is what is required to be completed each visit variable - changes from contract to contract
- Can the customer pause the service and then restart, or push and pull
- Does the contract have seasonality built into it?

## FSM Scheduling Process

- If Tasks are not created as part of the PM case creation
- who creates the tasks and schedules the work?
- How is the determined what work needs to be scheduled?
- How is it decided who will be scheduled to do the work?
- Are staff scheduled as part of teams for PM jobs?
- What information do you need to see on the schedule board?
- Are there any notifications required to be sent when scheduling?

## FSM In the Field Process

- For each different role in the field using the Field Service mobile.  If yes to any of the questions below then capture details.
- Role 1 - eg Air Conditioning Technician
- What Contact information does the person need to see on the mobile?
- Does the role need to create Assets in the field?
- Does the role need to update existing Assets in the field?
- Does the role need to complete any maintenance checklists in the field?
- Does the role need to complete any other checklists in the field e.g safety?
- Does the role need to capture their time in the field?
- Does the role need to capture parts usage from their van in the field?
- If yes, are they always assigned the same vans?
- If more than one person works on the maintenance, do they all carry van stock?
- Does the role need to capture any expenses in the field?
- Does the role need to capture images/photos in the field?
- Does the role need to capture any other information in the field eg arrival and departure times, issues, etc?
- Are Signatures required to be captured in the field at the completion of the task/job?
- Is there a requirement to view the Service Report from the mobile?
- Is there a requirement to send the Service Report from the mobile?
- Are there specific contacts that the user needs to be able to see on the mobile?

## Job Completion

- When is the Maintenance Job considered completed?
- What is the process for invoicing after completion of the job?

## Service Reports

- Are service reports required to be generated for this use case? (request examples)
- If so, what reports and what information is to be presented
- Who do Service Reports need to be delivered to?

## Service History

- What information captured from this use case is required to be visible on the Service History on the mobile in the future?
