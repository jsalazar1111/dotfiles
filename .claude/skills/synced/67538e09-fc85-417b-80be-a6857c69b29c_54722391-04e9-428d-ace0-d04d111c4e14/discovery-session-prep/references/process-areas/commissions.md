# Commissions — Discovery Questions

*Source: Discovery Questions Combined (NetSuite implementation discovery bank)*


## Sales Organization Structure

- Please describe your current sales department distribution.
- Do you have Inside sales reps? Do you have different job positions for them per tenure in the company?
- Do you work with third party sales agencies or contractors?
- Please outline sales targets and general commission structure. Do these targets vary between internal and external sales reps?
- How are you currently tracking commissions?
- Is there an approval process for commissions?
- How often are commissions paid?
- Are commissions based on Delivery Date or Collection Date?
- Are customer returns deducted from future commissions?
- Are items such as shipping and taxes included in commission considerations?
- Are commissions limited to specific items? (e.g. specific class, only physical merchandise, etc.)
