# Order to Cash — Discovery Questions

*Source: Discovery Questions Combined (NetSuite implementation discovery bank)*


## Lead Generation

- Are Leads currently tracked by your organization? If so, is it being managed by a single system, or by individual sales reps?
- How are new leads generated? Do you use any websites or online forms to capture new leads?
- Do you ever prepare Estimates or Quotes for Leads?

## Customer Master

- Will NetSuite be the system of record for Customer data? Or is there another system integrated with Netsuite that keeps the customer data updated?
- Do the customers have a parent child relationship?
- How do you classify or categorize customers?
- Do you have customers that transact with multiple subsidiaries?
- Do you have prepaid only customers?
- Please describe the payment terms used in your organization.
- Do you manage credit limits for your customers? If so, do you enforce the credit limits to prevent orders from being created? Please describe your credit policies.
- Do you sell through distributors or Resellers? If so, do you fill the distributor name in your End user customer record?
- Are customers assigned to specific sales territories or Sales reps?
- Do you have customers that are also Vendors or partners?
- How many customers do you have?
- Would you want your customer to login to the platform to review Orders and Invoices?

## Contacts

- Do you keep information of different contacts in your customer record?
- What information do you store in the contact record?
- Do you use contact information to email transactions to different people inside your customer’s organization?
- How many contacts do you have?

## Order to Cash

- Please describe the different Sales Order scenarios in your business.
- What’s the volume of orders created or entered in your current system? Who is responsible for entry?
- How many lines of details are printed in your orders? Do you have a client-facing version of the orders while following an internal more detailed order layout?
- What is the general item pricing strategy? Is pricing quantity-based? Are pricing groups used? Is there Customer-specific pricing?
- Would Netsuite be the main system for Sales order creation or would you integrate other systems to feed order information to NetSuite?
- Do you have an approval process for new orders? If so, please describe each tier.
- Do you track revenue based on project progress? If so, please describe the process to calculate project progress.
- Where is item pricing managed? Do you use the following:
    - ●    Price levels
    - ●    Price groups
    - ●    Quantity based pricing

## Customer Deposit - Prepayments

- Do you access prepayments for orders? If you do, please elaborate if you accept a fixed amount or a percentage of the total amount.
- Are deposits associated to one single order, or to multiple of them?

## Item fulfillment

- Will all item fulfillments be processed through NetSuite? Or will there be a third party platform involved?
- Do you use ship confirmation emails when an order is fulfilled?
- Is the fulfillment process broken down into distinct pick, pack, and ship stages, or are orders merely marked as shipped? If the former, will these stages need to be represented and tracked by order in NetSuite?
- Are picking tickets and/or packing slips utilized in the fulfillment process? Are there special requirements around timing or format for these documents?
- Which printed materials are included in shipments to customers? Invoice, picking tickets, etc.?
- Are there any further printed forms used or related to the fulfillment process?
- Which carriers do you use as part of your shipping process?
- Do you perform partial shipments? What is the process for this, and how are these orders tracked today?
- Will you be using the live UPS/FedEx integrator tool?
- Do you process shipments on customers’ UPS/FedEx accounts? (ie. 3rd party billing)
- Do you have standardized box sizes to be used during the shipping process?
- Do you track item weight?
- Do you ship any hazardous, fragile items?
- Do you own your own shipping trucks?

## Invoicing

- Please Describe your Invoicing process and its dependencies.
- Do you Invoice at the moment that products are shipped or when services are rendered? If not, please describe the steps that will originate the creation of a new invoice.
- Do you bill customers over a range of time or a contract term?
- Will you be using finance charges for late invoices?
- Do you create one Invoice per order? If not, please describe the process to create different Invoices per Order.
- Are invoices going to be sent via email to customers at the moment of creation?
- Do you create Standalone invoices, without a Sales order or Project?
- Would a single template be used for Outbound transactions? If not, provide a list of transaction types that require different PDF templates to be printed and sent to customers
- Do you group / consolidate invoices? If so, what are the criteria for doing this?

## Payments

- Do you charge customers using Credit cards? If so, do you store this information in your system of record or in another platform?
- What Payment methods do you accept for your customers?
- Do you receive payments in multiple currencies?
- What payment gateway product is currently in use, if any?

## Return to Credit

- Discuss the current process for authorizing, receiving, and refunding customer returns.
- In what use-cases will a customer return authorization be generated? Is there an approval process?
- Who is responsible for receiving returns? Are defective items accepted, or written off?
- Are refunds issued to customers, or credit memos? Both?
- Are refunds provided in advance of receiving a return?
- Are returns automatically returned to stock by default? Is there a process for this?

## Collections

- How are overdue balances and collections handled?
- Is there an annual percentage rate of interest applied to overdue payments?  If so, what is it?
- What is the amount of grace period days (if any) applied to overdue balances?
- Are charges assessed based on due date or transaction date of the invoice?
- How are collection letters handled?  Are they sent in bulk or individually?
- Are there existing templates used for collection letters?  If so, please describe

## Commissions

- Describe your current commissions structure.
- Do you require the ability to automate the process of calculating commission amounts, recording the corresponding journal entries, and issuing payment to employees for commissions?
- Who gets paid commissions? Sales People? Partners? 1099 Employees?

## Partners

- Do you have partners or distributors that create orders on your organization's behalf? (i.e. Sales Dealerships)
- Do you invoice your partners directly and add the customer as the end user?
- Would you have a partner accessing Netsuite to follow orders and other transactions?

## Anything missing?

- Are there any unique requirements or expectations that you have which have not been discussed or questioned above?
