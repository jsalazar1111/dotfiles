# Call to Resolution — Discovery Questions

*Source: Discovery Questions Combined (NetSuite implementation discovery bank)*


## Support Organization Structure

- Discuss your current support organization and overall structure.
- Who is responsible for opening support cases?
- Do you keep track of timesheets for support staff?
- Do you bill back any of the time performed for support activities?
- What metrics do you keep track of to monitor support organization performance?
- Do you maintain a knowledge base for your support staff?

## Support Cases

- How are support cases opened?
- Call in?
- Email in?
- Submitted online?
- Submitted through the customer  center?
- What means do support staff use to communicate with customers for cases?
- Who can respond to cases/assigned to cases? Only support staff or can any employee be assigned?
- NetSuite has the following standard case types: Question, Concern and Problem.
    - ●    Any reason this will not work?
- NetSuite has the following standard case priorities – High, Medium and Low.
    - ●     Do these naming conventions match your terminology or do you require different priorities?
    - ●    Do you have multiple websites with different branding, from names, and email addresses?
    - ●    Do you have different identities for replying to support cases?
    - ●    Do you have different case capture email addresses?
- NetSuite has the following standard case statuses – Not Started, In Progress, Escalated, Re-Opened, and Closed.
    - ●    Do these naming conventions match your terminology or do you require different statuses?

## Support Assignment

- After you've defined your case types, case origins, support issues and support items, you can set up rules and territories to automatically assign cases to support reps or support groups. When you create a case rule, you set criteria for a certain field on the case record. You then group rules into territories and assign a support rep to the territory. Any new cases that meet the criteria of each rule in a territory are assigned to that support rep.
    - ○    Name of territory?
    - ○    Rules for territory?
    - ○    Reps that fall into each territory?
    - ●    Can a Support rep  log into NetSuite and simply grab an open case awaiting assignment
    - ●    Do you provide pre-defined solutions for common problems for your support reps to utilize in case resolution?
