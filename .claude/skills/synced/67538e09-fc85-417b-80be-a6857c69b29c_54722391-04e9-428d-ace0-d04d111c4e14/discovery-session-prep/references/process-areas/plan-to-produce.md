# Plan to Produce — Discovery Questions

*Source: Discovery Questions Combined (NetSuite implementation discovery bank)*


## Bills of Materials Overview

- When defining component items in a BOM, what are typically included: Inventory, Non-Inventory, Other Charge, Service, and Assembly Items?
- Are any of the component items lot-controlled or serialized items?
- Do you design builds by unit or by batch? If by unit, do you attach a Serial Number to the final assembled item?  If by batch, do you track the batch by lot number?
- What is your process for changes in your BOMs? Do you use revision numbering for BOM modifications?
- Is revision control utilised for Bill of Materials to track and control component and process changes over time?
- Do you currently use a BOM effective date or expiration/end date?
- If you build your assemblies at different locations, do you use the same BOM for all locations?
- How are component items sourced? (Stock, Purchase Order, Work Order)
- Are bins labelled with a scan-able barcode?
- How are bins organized in the facility (e.g. primary pick area; overflow area; velocity-based; item attributes (frozen, security cage, etc.), unit of measure, other)?
- Are items replenished from overflow / backstock to primary / preferred bins?

## Work Order Overview

- Are assemblies made to order (Special Work Order), made to stock (Production Work Order), or both?
- What triggers are used to generate work orders? (i.e. Sales Demand, below safety stock, etc)
- For multi-level assemblies, when a work order is generated for the top level item, do you automatically generate work orders to build the sub-assembly items?
- Do you contract work orders out? Are there operations that are defined as outside operations?

## Routings Overview

- Do you currently use routings?
- How many processes does an average assembly go through?
- What is the average length of time it takes to complete a finished assembly (from entry of work order to completion)?
- Are any of the steps in your routing schedules subcontracted to a 3rd party?
- Do you utilize the same routing schedule for a single assembly item across all locations?  Can the routing for a single assembly item vary between different locations?
- How do you track labor setup time for a manufacturing task?
- How do you track labor run rates for a manufacturing task?
- How do you track machine setup time for a manufacturing task?
- How do you track machine run rates for a manufacturing task?
- Do you track your overhead costs on your work orders?
- Do you have work centers?
- How many shifts and days of the week do you work?  What is the duration of the shift?
- Will you track and record WIP?
