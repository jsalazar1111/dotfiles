# Grant Management — Discovery Questions

*Source: Discovery Questions Combined (NetSuite implementation discovery bank)*


## Grant Management

- How many grants are you tracking? Please distinguish between federal transfers and any other grant types.
- How often / are any of your grants a pledge or receivable?
- How often / are any of your grants reimbursable that require expenses to be billed to the government or a granting entity?
- What kind of restrictions do you have on your grants (if any)?
- Do many or any of your grants have a parent/child relationship (i.e. one major grant with subgrants that need to be tracked individually)?
- Standard fields on the grant record include:
    - Title
    - Parent
    - Grant Description
    - Sponsor
    - Total Amount
    - Start Date / End Date
    - Status
    - Grant Type
    - Grant Restriction
    - Grant Term
    - Grant Priority
    - Relationship Manager
    - Program Officer
    - Grant Lead
    - Grant Application Date
    - Grant Application Decision Date
    - Grant Inquiry Date
    - Grant Inquiry Decision Date
- What other fields are important to track, or are not important to you?
- Do grants fund multiple departments/programs? If so, how? For example, does the federal grant transfer need to also be allocated across multiple departments?
- What milestones, if any, do you need to track for each grant? What happens at each milestone/milestone type (i.e., does this indicate an invoicing period to a granting entity or the government)?
- How do you need to use blanket POs for grant budgeting purposes?
- What, if any, kinds of allocations are required for expenses to your grants (departmental expenses, salaries, etc.)?
- How are you tracking / do you need to track grant budgets? What kind of reporting do you need per grant?
- Standard reporting for grants includes:
    - Statement of Activity
    - Grant Statement of Activity
    - Grant Expenses by Year
    - Grant Expense Detail
    - Net Assets by Grant
    - Overdue Grant Payments
    - Upcoming Grant Milestones Report
    - Unbilled Grants Report
- Are there additional reporting or document storage requirements?
- Are there any other intricacies to the grants you manage that will provide further detail to the set up?
- Anything else not covered above?
