## Fixed Asset  Management

- How many Fixed Assets do you currently have?
- Where do you currently track asset depreciation?
- What are the categories of assets you have? Please list them
- Example:
- • Machinery & Equipment
- • Buildings
- • Computer Equipment
- • Furniture & Fixtures
- • Leasehold Improvements
- • Vehicles
- Do you have assets in all your subsidiaries? If so, are assets transferred from one subsidiary to another?
- How do you enter new assets in your current system?
- Do you build any of your assets as part of your manufacturing process?
- Do you go through the process of splitting assets?
- (Assets should be split when one component of an asset needs to be tracked separately from the other components)
- If using Multi-book, is Fixed Assets depreciation needed for a secondary set of books (per book depreciation)?
- What depreciation methods do you use?
    - ●    Straight line depreciation only
    - ●    Another method. Indicate all that apply:
    - ○    Sum of Years Digits,
    - ○    Asset Usage,
    - ○    Fixed Declining,
    - ○    Straight Line Remaining,
    - ○    Sum of Years/ Straight Line
    - ○    150DB &amp
    - ○    200DB,
    - ○    Other Custom Method
- Do you need to track Alternate depreciation for any of your assets?
    - -     Tax Depreciation is the most common form of alternate depreciation.  Assigning a tax method enables corporate reporting on the asset that can be used to generate special tax or audit reports.
- Lease Accounting needed?
- ​​• NetSuite Fixed Asset Management module is compliant with lease accounting regulations such as IFRS 16 & ASC 842. This is achieved by running the asset revaluation manually.
- • Utilize delivered reports such as expiring leased assets, short-term leased liabilities and long-term liabilities for full visibility of leased assets.
- Do you need an approval process when creating new assets?
- How often do you run asset depreciation?
- When you dispose of assets, would you write them off or sell them?
- Will you import only active assets or will you import historical accumulated depreciation for older assets as well (e.g. 500 assets by 1,000 assets on
    - books).
- How many historical assets will you be
- importing (e.g. assets that have a depreciation balance?)
