## Common Objections and Responses

### "Why not BDO, RSM, or Grant Thornton?"

Those firms are accounting practices that do NetSuite implementations on the side. Their delivery teams are strong on the finance and compliance side and weaker on the technical configuration, integration, and AI-readiness side. Plative is a technology consulting firm first. For customers who need ARM configuration, Workato integrations, AI agents, or a NetSuite-Salesforce connection, a traditional accounting SI will scope it incorrectly and underdeliver on execution.

### "Why not Deloitte or Accenture?"

Same answer as on the Salesforce side: those firms win on brand for very large enterprise deals. For mid-market companies, they over-resource the sale and staff the delivery with their most junior team. Plative brings senior practitioners from day one. The people who sold the engagement are reachable throughout delivery.

### "We have never heard of Plative."

Plative is a Top-3 Oracle NetSuite Alliance Partner in North America with 3,000+ completed projects and five NetSuite Spotlight Awards from a single season. We are intentionally focused on mid-market and growth-stage companies, which means we are not competing for the Fortune 100 deals. We are competing for the accounts in your book that the big SIs would staff with their least experienced people anyway.

### "The customer is already using QuickBooks and does not want to change."

QuickBooks works until it does not. The signal to watch for: multi-entity reporting pain, ASC 606 compliance requirements, PE involvement, or a headcount threshold where month-end close takes longer than a week. When any of those are present, the conversation shifts from "should we change" to "when and with whom." Plative has run QuickBooks-to-NetSuite migrations at speed and can anchor the business case for the CFO.

### "The customer's budget is tight."

Plative's blended delivery model (senior NA-based architects, offshore delivery capacity in Mumbai and Manila) is specifically designed for mid-market budget realities. The AI-native delivery approach also reduces billable hours relative to a traditional implementation. Tight budget is a reason to bring Plative in, not a reason to delay the conversation.

### "The customer had a failed NetSuite implementation before."

This is one of the best signals for Plative. Failed implementations almost always have the same root causes: scope that was not properly defined upfront, ARM configured incorrectly, or integrations that were bolted on rather than designed in. Plative's pre-engagement diagnostic process surfaces exactly what went wrong and scopes the remediation with specificity. We have rebuilt NetSuite environments for customers who were burned by a prior SI.
