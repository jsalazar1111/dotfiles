Brand Guidelines · 02.2026

Icons are linear, on a 24px grid, drawn at 1.6px stroke with rounded caps. Always single-color — Navy on light backgrounds, white on dark. Never fill an icon with crimson; reserve crimson for accents on the surrounding UI.

Anatomy

Built on a 24-unit grid with a 2-unit safe area. Stroke 1.6px, rounded caps and joins. No fills, no double-weight strokes.

24 × 24 grid
2u safe area
1.6px stroke
Round caps

Sizing

16, 20, 24 and 32 px in product UI. 36–48 px in slides. Always pixel-snapped on display.

Color rule

Single color, matched to surrounding text. Crimson is reserved for accent UI — never for icon strokes.
